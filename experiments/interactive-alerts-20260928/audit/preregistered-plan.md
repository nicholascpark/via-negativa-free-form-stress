# Preregistered audit plan

Registered before reviewing the public/private fixture, practitioner dialogue, or candidate outcome.

## Protocol
- Remain independent: no coaching, expectations, implementation advice, or evaluations to the practitioner or simulated user. Report fatal fixture defects only to the orchestrator.
- Read the frozen skill and public/private setup after the orchestrator freezes it. Check feasibility and whether private expected outcomes follow from stated facts and commitments.
- Freeze any independently derived holdout or metamorphic cases before viewing the final candidate. Do not invent requirements or use practitioner outcomes to choose expectations.
- Wait for explicit `candidate frozen`; execute independent checks against a frozen copy in this audit directory. Keep practitioner checks and auditor checks distinct.
- Use pass / partial / fail / untestable judgments, concrete evidence, and scope qualifications. One scripted human simulation cannot establish comparative superiority or real-user effectiveness.

## Criteria and evidence
1. Information value: reconstruct each question, what was unavailable before it, the reply, and whether the revealed information could affect a committed obligation.
2. Precommitment and adaptation: inspect preserved before-answer snapshots for a question-to-action mapping; trace reply to decision and actual artifact changes.
3. Interaction quality: assess leading wording, examples, timing, repetition, forced choices, and unnecessary burden using the entire observed dialogue.
4. Development continuity: look for concrete representations or working artifacts advancing throughout the interaction, beyond suggestion lists.
5. Local tests: identify worker-generated cases grounded in actual obligations and their expectation sources; separate these from independent tests.
6. Artifact fidelity: test public commitments, revealed commitments, and withheld private cases against the frozen artifact; report every material failure.
7. Evidence honesty: check claims against executed evidence, disclose simulation and test limits, and flag unsupported generalizations.
8. Cost: report observed question/reply rounds, interruptions, available timing or tool counts, and explicit limits of cost measurement.

## Deliverable
Write concise `audit.md` with traceable question → answer → decision → artifact → check chains, actual executed results, unresolved issues, criterion judgments, and a bounded conclusion about whether this trial supports useful behavior.
