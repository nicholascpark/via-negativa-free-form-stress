# Coordinator mathematical review

This review inspects the externally stated arguments in DERIVATION-v1.md. It is not an audit of private reasoning and not proof-assistant verification. Findings are withheld from the practitioner during the episode.

The character expectation calculation is valid: uniform averaging kills a nonzero linear functional, and the remaining signs depend on the right-hand side through the dual space. Fourier characters on each Boolean subcube determine that marginal. This gives the claimed minimum dual-support threshold, including an empty minimum and zero-dimensional cases.

For a connected graph, an incidence-dual vector is constant on adjacent vertices and therefore constant globally. The rank and parity-class counts follow. The prism construction has degree three, and each incident variable appears in four clauses at each of its two endpoints. The clause count and occurrence count are consistent. The theorem correctly distinguishes parity-block marginals from individual-clause marginals.

The partition identity follows by expanding the product of the two-term expression for t^v. Its factor 2^(n-m) can be fractional when m exceeds n; the resulting expression still has integer coefficients. The independent checker includes such rectangular cases and uses exact rational arithmetic. The derivative-at-one moment argument and thermal contrast expression are consistent with the stated finite polynomial identities. These are not computational lower bounds.

The general-SAT transfer remains missing. The seven-point satisfying set of an ordinary three-variable disjunction cannot be one affine coset because a nonempty affine coset has power-of-two size. This is an obstruction to that representation, not to arbitrary polynomial algorithms. The practitioner's explicit warning about compact high-degree parity is essential.

The coordinator wrote a separate implementation without importing worker code, after reading the claimed identities. It checked 146 small matrices, 22,023 marginal-threshold comparisons, and 4,108 partition evaluations, all exactly. This is developmental verification of finite cases, not hidden holdout evidence, a universal proof, independent skill efficacy, or evidence of novelty.
