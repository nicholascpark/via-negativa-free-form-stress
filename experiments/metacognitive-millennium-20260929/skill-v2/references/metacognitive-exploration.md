# Assumption revision as an executable research practice

Use this for a foundational problem, a stuck representation, or an explicit
request for metacognitive or revelatory exploration. The output is a changed
research object and a defensible account of its consequences, not a claim to
experience altered consciousness. External definitions, equations, experiments
and decision summaries suffice; do not request private chains of thought.

## Find the assumptions that are doing work

Freeze a short target card with the source, exact question, quantifiers, domains,
acceptable outputs, and what the user hopes to understand. Distinguish the formal
task from a broader motivating question. Meeting one need not settle the other.

Use a compact assumption map only for consequential entries:

| Kind | What it means | How to treat it |
|---|---|---|
| Target requirement | A condition in the actual requested statement | Preserve on the target route; label variants separately |
| Definition or convention | What an object, size, equality or measurement means | Change with an explicit correspondence, including lost information |
| Established dependency | A theorem, observation or verified constraint being used | Record source and applicability, not just its name |
| Working hypothesis | An unproved condition supporting a branch | State conditional conclusions and how this condition could fail |
| Representational habit | A symmetry, decomposition, coordinate choice or search order imposed by us | A promising first place to vary or remove a constraint |

For each consequential entry, identify the step that uses it. Try one deletion,
weakening, reversal or substitution and observe what stops working or becomes
possible. Mark untested entries as untested. Do not equate fewer written
assumptions with less mathematical content: an innocuous-looking regularity,
positivity, compression or closure condition may encode the desired result.

“Minimal assumptions” is relative to a construction and a dependency set. A
counterexample to a proposed weakening shows that weakening fails in its stated
scope. Failure to find a proof without a hypothesis does not show necessity.
Foundational axiom minimization is a separate task unless explicitly requested.

## Alternate expansive construction with precise return

Set a bounded generative interval and reserve time to inspect its products.
For a short first cycle, two contrasting branches with a few transformations
each are a useful default, not a universal quota. Do not require a useful
application after every association. Keep requirements visible without forcing
the branch to remain in its original coordinates.

Possible departures, chosen because they unlock an operation:

- Exchange what is constructed and what is prescribed. Track the admissibility
  conditions of the quantity that becomes free.
- Replace an aggregate by a relation, distribution, geometry or history. Exhibit
  cases the old summary merged and the new representation distinguishes.
- Break an imposed symmetry; derive the interaction it had suppressed.
- Explore a counterfactual or restricted world, then seek the exact obstruction
  to carrying its mechanism back.
- Treat the current defect as an object to represent or realize rather than as a
  scalar to minimize. Recompute interactions created by each correction.
- Use a distant visual or verbal analogy to propose an operator. Translate the
  analogy into defined inputs, outputs and a nontrivial example before relying on it.

Keep ancestry in a notebook or the existing research archive. A compact record:

```text
Parent / target version:
Assumption added, removed or reinterpreted:
Requirements retained:
New construction and operation performed:
What this enables that the parent did not:
Status: exact / conjectural / restricted / counterfactual
Evidence or failure witness:
Remaining return obligation and next operation:
Decision this operation could change; result that would redirect the branch:
```

These are research records, not introspection. A branch can explore a false
counterfactual knowingly; another branch must not inherit it as established fact.

At the checkpoint, ask whether the branch created an actionable distinction,
construction or lemma. A familiar result can be valuable as a stepping stone;
do not label rediscovery as novelty. Preserve an unsuccessful construction if it
exposes a reusable witness family. If only the metaphor changed, change the
operation, not its rhetoric. Stop or redirect a branch that repeats the same
obstruction without developing an artifact.

## Convert an insight into obligations

Write the smallest precise claim suggested by the construction. Identify its
inputs, output, dependencies and a witness or limiting case that might defeat it.
Check each implication toward the original target, not merely the new claim in
isolation. Distinguish:

- A proved equivalence: both directions under stated definitions.
- A sufficient condition: proving it would settle the target, but it remains open.
- A restricted result: valid in a specified class with a missing transfer.
- A heuristic: a proposed reason to investigate, not an implication.

Trace a dependency loop when a new hypothesis is alleged to be simpler. If the
argument assumes a capability equivalent to the target, keep that equivalence
as a map of the obstruction and seek an independently accessible sublemma.
Do not count a renamed target as progress toward its proof.

Name a next operation with an input and an expected kind of output: derive an
estimate with its constants, construct a witness family, compare two defined
models, or establish a preservation lemma. “Prove the missing theorem” is an
unresolved objective, not yet a research operation. Apply known impossibility or
barrier results only to the hypotheses they actually cover; a barrier may
restrict a proof method without forbidding exploration of the problem.

Before investing in a continuation, explain what its possible outcomes change:
which transfer obstacle would become more accessible, what would justify further
work, and what would redirect this route. Scope a negative verdict to the tested
representation or mechanism. More special cases or bigger enumerations alone
need not advance the original question. This is an allocation decision at the
checkpoint, not a demand to justify every association during generation.

For mathematical work, informal derivation, symbolic computation, numerical
sampling and proof-assistant checking support different claims. If formalization
is available, compare the encoded statement and assumptions with the target,
and record the actual checker command and outcome. Without a run, say so.

## Learn from a surprising result without inventing its cause

Use the result to update the assumption map and next choice. Record what changed
your decision: a derived identity, a counterexample, a retrieved theorem, a user
answer or a computation. Attribution to a prompt technique requires comparisons;
a vivid experience or a successful external paper does not supply that evidence.

The [Navier–Stokes case](navier-stokes-alignment.md) motivates distinguishing
admissible degrees of freedom from habits, and turning residuals into
constructions. These are interpretations of visible artifacts. They do not
reconstruct the researchers' hidden discovery process, guarantee convergence,
or imply that three agents reproduce the resources of that research effort.

Keep the surviving operator with assumptions, tested scope, known failures and
its next use. Integration can yield a new question or representation as well as
a result. Save an unresolved return obligation rather than erasing it in the
summary. Use [fresh-context trials](fresh-context-trials.md) when evaluating the
skill itself.
