Retrieved 2026-09-29. Only primary research sources below are relied on; no repository, previous episode, observer, or baseline artifact was read.

1. Stephen Cook, *The P versus NP Problem*, official Clay statement: https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf . Used for the formal verifier target and SAT completeness. Historical algorithm bounds and hardware comments in the document are not treated as current facts.
2. Toniann Pitassi and Robert Robere, *Strongly Exponential Lower Bounds for Monotone Computation*, ECCC Report 188 (2016): https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2016/188/download/ . Its graph-parity definitions confirm that the sparse construction is the established Tseitin family. Its use of lifting to obtain bounds for restricted monotone models supplies context for why an independent transfer theorem matters. None of its lower bounds is proved anew or claimed by this episode.
