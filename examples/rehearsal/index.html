Does this talk need a teleprompter?

The request is a browser teleprompter. First, make one cheap comparison: deliver the same short talk from a manuscript and from cues. Then choose what deserves to be built.

Two legitimate needs: preserving exact delivery, or helping a speaker prepare and improvise. Trying both can expose a different problem; it can also confirm the original one.
